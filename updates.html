Short updates on the Manux website, app and games. Newest first.

17 Nov 2026

New name and logo: Manux

  • The site is now called Manux, with a new logo and share image.
  • Daily Code: a new secret colour code every day, with streaks.
  • Snakes & Ladders now supports 2, 3 or 4 players.
20 Nov 2026

Sudoku: 12 types

Classic, Mini 6×6, Kids 4×4, Fruit 6×6, Diagonal X, Hyper, Jigsaw, Anti-Knight, Anti-King, Non-Consecutive, Even-Odd and Killer. Every puzzle has exactly one answer. Now with pencil notes and undo.

27 Nov 2026

Sharper arcade games

Flappy, Pong, Breakout, Stack Tower, Shooter, Dodge, Catch and Runner now draw in high resolution, so shapes and text look crisp on phone screens. Flappy has a new sunset look with an animated bird, Dodge has a starfield, spinning rocks and explosions, and Breakout has glossy bricks, particles and a ball trail, all with new sounds.

26 Nov 2026

15 new games

Asteroid Blaster, Zombie Yard, Sky Hop, Maze Muncher, Fruit Slice, Neon Racer, Block Blast, Bubble Pop, Gem Match, Fruit Merge, Hoops, Penalty Kick, Super Over, Tower Defense and Solitaire. Smooth sharp graphics, new sound effects and touch controls. Now 68 games.

22 Nov 2026

Safer exit from games

Pressing "All games" or your phone's back button now asks "Leave this game?" first, so a slip of the thumb no longer loses your game, and the game pauses while it asks. Games are also centred on the screen.

22 Nov 2026

Bounce: level picker

Tap ☰ Levels to see all 60 levels. Cleared levels are marked with a tick and can be replayed any time. The ball now swims up slowly in water.

21 Nov 2026

Bounce: 11 more traps

Moving and vanishing platforms over wide gaps, rolling spiked boulders, sliding spikes, spinning spike wheels, moving walls, shrinking spikes, electric barriers, falling rocks, spring traps and narrow tunnels.

21 Nov 2026

Bounce: six new traps

Crumbling brick bridges over pits, slippery ice, swinging spiked maces, fire jets, laser beams and ceiling crushers. Time your run past each one. Levels now get steadily harder all the way to level 60.

21 Nov 2026

Bounce: 60 levels, much longer

Bounce now has 60 levels instead of 30, and every level is about five times as long as before, with many more rings, spikes, spiders and pits. You can also leap out of water onto high ledges.

19 Nov 2026

New game: Bounce

Now with red-brick levels on a blue sky, water pools you swim through, spiders, spike posts, flat and upright rings, 💎 extra lives and a pause button. Roll the red ball through 60 levels. It bounces by itself, you jump higher on demand, collect every ring to open the gate, dodge spikes and spiky balls, and use springs and checkpoint flags. Has a Retro LCD look too.

19 Nov 2026

Battle Royale in 3D

Third-person 3D view with Free Fire style controls: left stick to move, drag the right side to look, big fire button, scope button.

18 Nov 2026

New game: Battle Royale

Drop from a plane onto one of 4 maps (Greenfield, Dune Sea, Frostbite, Neon City). Loot 22 guns with 2x, 4x and 8x scopes, silencers, extended mags and lasers, plus armour, medkits, grenades, gloo walls and airdrops. Outlast the bots as the safe zone shrinks. Every game also now opens on its own full screen, and Ludo has a new look.
